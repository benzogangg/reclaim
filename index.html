Where the SOL comes from

Stake and buffers

A stake account you deactivated keeps its SOL until you withdraw it. Buffers left over from program deployments hold their whole deposit until the authority closes them.

Rewards and creator fees

Launchpads and pools collect fees for token creators and liquidity providers. They sit in a vault until the owner claims them.

Account rent

Every token you ever held got an account with a rent deposit of about 0.002 SOL. Closing the empty ones returns it; wrapped SOL is unwrapped to plain SOL.

What you sign

  • Everything goes to your wallet. There is no fee and no transfer to anyone else. This site has no program of its own and no server; it runs entirely in your browser.
  • Checked twice. Every item is simulated on Solana before it is shown, and the page refuses to send a transaction unless each instruction is exactly one it built for an item from your scan. Your wallet shows its own preview before you approve.
  • Nothing is burned unless you pick it. Burning NFTs or leftover tokens is opt-in, item by item.
  • No keys. The page never asks for a seed phrase or private key.
